Stranger Things is not a movie; it is a globally popular American science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix.

Here is a summary of the key information:

Overview of the TV Series
Genre: Science fiction, horror, drama, mystery, coming-of-age.

Setting: The fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, primarily set in the 1980s.

Plot: The series begins in 1983 with the disappearance of a young boy, Will Byers. His friends, family, and the local police chief are drawn into an extraordinary mystery involving top-secret government experiments, terrifying supernatural forces, and a mysterious young girl with psychokinetic abilities named Eleven. The main conflict revolves around a hostile alternate dimension called the Upside Down.



Creators/Showrunners: The Duffer Brothers (Matt and Ross Duffer).

Starring: A large ensemble cast including Winona Ryder, David Harbour, Finn Wolfhard, Millie Bobby Brown, Gaten Matarazzo, Caleb McLaughlin, Noah Schnapp, Natalia Dyer, Charlie Heaton, and Joe Keery.

Key Details & Seasons
Original Release Date: July 15, 2016 (Season 1)

Platform: Netflix Original Series

Seasons Released:

Season 1 (2016)

Season 2 (2017)

Season 3 (2019)

Season 4 (2022)

Final Season: Season 5 is the final season of the main series.
